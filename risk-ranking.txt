Task 1: Threat Model and Secure Configuration

Step 3: Risk Ranking

Application: Login Web Application

Objective:
To prioritize the potential threats identified in Step 2 based on likelihood and impact.

Scoring method:
Likelihood: 1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High
Impact:     1 = Low, 2 = Medium, 3 = High
Risk Score = Likelihood x Impact

Risk levels:
1-2 = Low
3-4 = Medium
6-9 = High

Risk Register

1. Spoofing
Threat: An attacker uses stolen login credentials.
Likelihood: 3 (High)
Impact: 3 (High)
Risk score: 9
Risk level: High
Reason: Compromised credentials may allow unauthorized account access.

2. Tampering
Threat: An attacker modifies user data without authorization.
Likelihood: 2 (Medium)
Impact: 3 (High)
Risk score: 6
Risk level: High
Reason: Unauthorized changes may affect data integrity.

3. Repudiation
Threat: Insufficient audit logs make user actions difficult to trace.
Likelihood: 2 (Medium)
Impact: 2 (Medium)
Risk score: 4
Risk level: Medium
Reason: Missing logs can make investigations and accountability difficult.

4. Information Disclosure
Threat: Personal information is exposed to unauthorized users.
Likelihood: 2 (Medium)
Impact: 3 (High)
Risk score: 6
Risk level: High
Reason: Exposure may harm user privacy and confidentiality.

5. Denial of Service (DoS)
Threat: Excessive requests overwhelm the login server.
Likelihood: 2 (Medium)
Impact: 3 (High)
Risk score: 6
Risk level: High
Reason: The application may become unavailable to legitimate users.

6. Elevation of Privilege
Threat: A normal user gains unauthorized administrator access.
Likelihood: 2 (Medium)
Impact: 3 (High)
Risk score: 6
Risk level: High
Reason: Unauthorized admin access could allow sensitive actions.

Conclusion:
Based on this illustrative assessment, spoofing is prioritized as High risk. Tampering, information disclosure, denial of service, and elevation of privilege are also High risk. Repudiation is Medium risk. These ratings are estimates for a sample login application and should be updated if actual application details or evidence become available.

Note:
Likelihood and impact scores are illustrative assumptions, not measured findings from a real application.
